Closing a deal is often treated as the defining milestone in a merger or acquisition, representing months, sometimes years, of strategic work, negotiation and planning. And yet, it is only one step in a much longer journey. It's also where deal value is captured or lost, depending on whether people adopt the new ways of working required by the integration.
